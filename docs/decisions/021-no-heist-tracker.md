# 021: No heist cooldown tracker, no Heists tab

2026-10-15, issue #12.

## Decision

- **No heist cooldowns are tracked**, and the Heists tab is removed. An old `#/heists` link
  opens the default tab.
- The research (sources on issue #12) found one cooldown worth a timer: Cayo Perico, 144 minutes
  solo or 48 with a crew, on real time. Every other heist has 48 minutes with a crew and no
  sourced solo value, or no cooldown at all.
- **Not modelled:** the weekly completion bonus from The Kortz Center Heist update (more money for
  the first finale each week), and the 7-day Cayo Perico period with worse loot. The second
  would need its own separate tracker.

## Why

A list for one real cooldown is not worth a tab. A new issue can bring the tab back if one of the
items above turns out to be worth tracking.
